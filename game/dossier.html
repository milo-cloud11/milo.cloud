HOLLOW.

A short horror world for VRChat. Designed, built and scripted solo in Unity 2022.3 with UdonSharp.

Enter Hollow on VRChat

The listing opens in any browser.
The VRChat client is required to enter the world.

The world

Hollow drops visitors on a moonlit shore at night. The only light leads to a doorway that should not be open. Inside, a skeleton keeps watch over a glowing red skull. A keypad puzzle gates the path deeper, and the skull can be picked up and carried. Past the puzzle, a hidden theater plays a horror film. The world favors atmosphere over startle, with sound, fog and lighting tuned for slow dread.
